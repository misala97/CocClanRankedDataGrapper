B — one lane, exactly the row chart at panel scale

No lanes at all: price line rides the upper band INSIDE the same canvas as the chatter body, exactly like the open-market rows. Maximum consistency, and divergence is one picture. Cost: with 3Y of price and 9 days of chatter, the violet body occupies the last 2% of a shared canvas — the long spans become a price chart with a violet speck, which is the exact thing the two-lane split was built to avoid. C — chatter leads, price demoted to a ribbon

Inverts the hierarchy: the product's own signal gets the big lane, price becomes a thin sentiment-ribbon strip underneath (still green/red by direction, still to scale within itself). Strongest statement of what the tool is. Cost: reading an actual price level off the ribbon is hard — you get shape, not values; the gutter prices go.
