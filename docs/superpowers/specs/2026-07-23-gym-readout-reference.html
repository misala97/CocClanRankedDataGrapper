Readout

Direction 02 gave the chassis: condensed industrial type, oversized numerals, machined edges, real press depth. Direction 03 gave the language: colour that means something, and a record that lands as a moment. What is honestly both is the display on a serious piece of equipment — a rowing monitor, a timing system, a scoreboard. The numbers glow because they are lit, not because a gradient was applied.

Desktop is not the phone stretched. It is a board: what has stopped moving, what is under-trained, whether total load is still climbing — the three things you actually plan from. Same type, same states, same edges.

What gets locked if you approve this:

  • Ground is near-black; surfaces are chassis panels with a lit top edge and a hard bottom edge. Radii stay small — machined, not soft.
  • Amber is now. Cyan is record. Red is attention. White is fact. No colour is decorative, and nothing else gets a hue.
  • Numerals are condensed, tabular, and the largest thing in any container. Labels are small, uppercase, and quiet.
  • Every state carries a shape or a word as well as a colour.
  • Motion is mechanical: bars fill linearly, counters tick, the record flares once and settles. No bounce.

Type here is a Windows stand-in. The real build uses one engineered family across three roles — condensed for display and numerals, regular for body, mono for dense tabular data — picked and tuned during implementation.
