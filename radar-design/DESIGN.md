# Radar visual direction — selected, 9 September 2026

Scope: standalone future Radar hub only. No Gym or Clash of Clans design authority.

Owner selection: A's light/green overview, B's research arrangement restyled into the same identity. C is rejected.

Physical context: a German user at a desktop in afternoon daylight, opening research before US trading and returning throughout the session. The owner explicitly prefers light surfaces with green accents.

Palette: deep pine navigation, neutral near-white canvas, white bounded surfaces, dark green actions, muted sage data marks. Red/amber communicate negative moves or evidence qualifications. One sans-serif family, Inter, with tabular financial figures. No decorative photography or slogans. Charts and plain-language evidence are the primary content.

Overview organizes the day as a prioritized reading experience. Research uses candidate / evidence / thesis columns when width permits; the chart and source evidence stay central. Supporting pages share navigation and components, not identical dashboard card layouts. Administration is separated under the main navigation.

Prototype files use scoped OKLCH tokens in preview.css. Mobile navigation is an expandable sidebar; research becomes a full-width sequential view. Minimum small-screen chart labels need visual verification whenever plot geometry changes.

This is an initial selected system, still subject to owner feedback on rendered pages. Do not treat visual QA as user approval of every page.
